Account and authentication information

Profile information

  • full name

  • account email

Content you create or upload

Location and place information

  • destination and place selections
  • addresses and place identifiers
  • latitude and longitude associated with plans or moments
  • location metadata we extract from uploaded media if you choose to upload files containing it

Social and collaboration information

  • follows and public profile interactions
  • collaborators on plans
  • invitations you send or receive, including invitee email addresses
  • shared review and plan participation details

Notifications and device/browser data

  • in-app notification records
  • browser notification permission state

Media and technical metadata

  • file name
  • content type
  • file size
  • image or video dimensions
  • duration and bitrate for videos
  • storage paths and processing status for uploads

Enquiry information

When you ask to be put in touch with a travel company, we collect:

  • your name, your email address and, if you give it, your telephone number

  • the travel companies or specialists you choose
  • for a transfer, the passenger and flight details you give

In any planning conversation we also collect an anonymous identifier that links your activity within a visit and when you return, and the name of the travel company whose planning experience you are using.

Demo bookings

  • the name, email address and any notes you enter when you book a demo with us through our Google Calendar booking page

Usage and analytics information

  • pages viewed, by their address up to the path plus any campaign tags (utm_ parameters, the labels on a link we or a partner published that say where a visit came from); nothing else from the address

  • on our website, the sections you read and the buttons you use, such as booking a demo or logging in

  • if you agree to Google Analytics, a random identifier stored in a cookie that recognises repeat visits from the same device, and for consultants signed in to our workspace, a random identifier for their account (see section 9)

Usage, diagnostics, and security information

  • logs needed to secure and operate the service
  • error, abuse-prevention, and performance information
  • chat/session metadata and recommendation-feedback signals

6. AI Processing

Tripcerto includes AI-powered features, including conversational travel planning and recommendation systems.

When you use those features, we may process:

  • prompts and messages you submit
  • trip context and preference information
  • documents you upload, which an AI provider reads in full to extract your itinerary: the document's own text where it has one, and otherwise the PDF or image itself

We may send relevant content to third-party AI providers and supporting tools that process data on our behalf in order to provide these features.

Tripcerto does not use your content to train its own proprietary models. Instead, we use third-party AI models and tools to help generate, organise, and deliver responses and workflows within the service.

Current AI providers reflected in the service include:

  • OpenAI
  • Anthropic

Voice input

If you speak to our planning assistant instead of typing, your browser's own speech recognition turns your speech into text before anything reaches us. Some browsers, such as Google Chrome, do this on their maker's servers, under that company's terms. We receive only the text.

9. Cookies, Local Storage, and Similar Technologies

Tripcerto uses browser-side storage and similar technologies to run the service, including session persistence and app-state recovery.

Tripcerto currently uses browser-side storage for functions such as:

  • local storage is used for authentication persistence
  • session storage is used for short-lived auth and recovery flow state
  • in our planning experiences, local storage holds an anonymous identifier, and a pointer to your latest conversation with its title, so you can pick up where you left off
  • on our website, local storage remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme (theme); it is set only when you change it
  • browser notification permissions may be requested when you use notification features

Cookies

  • tc_consent records your answer to our analytics question and when you gave it, so that we ask once across www.tripcerto.com, chat.tripcerto.com and workspace.tripcerto.com. It is kept for 6 months, after which we ask again. It is strictly necessary to remember your choice and holds nothing else.
  • tc_team is set only on our own staff's browsers, so that our own visits are left out of our analytics. It is kept for 6 months.
  • _ga and _ga_<id> are Google Analytics cookies, set only if you agree. They hold a random identifier that recognises repeat visits from the same device across our website and applications on tripcerto.com, and last up to 13 months.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics on our website and in our web applications to understand how they are used. It processes the pages viewed (their address up to the path, plus any campaign tags), referring sites, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and browser type, and on our website the sections you read and the buttons you use. It sets no cookies, stores no identifier in your browser and does not track you across other websites. Because nothing is stored on or read from your device, we do not ask first: we rely on our legitimate interest in measuring and improving the website and the service.

Google Analytics 4 runs only if you agree to it. The first time you visit, we ask whether we may measure how the site is used with Google Analytics. Until you answer, and if you say no, it is not loaded at all. If you agree, one Tripcerto Google Analytics property measures our website, our travel planning experiences and our consultant workspace. It receives the pages you view and the page you came from, each as its address up to the path plus any campaign tags (utm_ parameters), and nothing else from the address; the sections you read and the buttons you use; your device type and whether the page is laid out for a phone, a tablet or a desktop; and your approximate location, which Google works out from your IP address without storing the address. For consultants signed in to our workspace, it also receives a random identifier for their account, so that their use is counted once across devices. It never receives your name, your email address or the content of a conversation. Its advertising features and Google signals are switched off, and nothing it collects is used for advertising. Google keeps the data for 14 months, and processes it in the United States under Google's data processing terms and the safeguards they provide.

Where a travel company that offers our planning experience has its own Google Analytics property, the events from that experience also go to that company's property, under the same answer; that company decides how long it keeps them.

Where our planning experience is embedded in a travel company's own website, we follow the choice you made in that website's cookie banner and do not ask again; if the website tells us nothing, we treat it as a no. Inside an embedded experience Google Analytics stores nothing on your device, and uses the identifier the travel company's own website already holds, where it passes it to us.

If you arrive at a planning experience through a partner's referral link, we store a short code identifying the referrer on your device, again only if you agree; until then it is held only for the page you are on. The code identifies the referrer, not you.

We do not use advertising or cross-site marketing technologies.

Changing your mind

Choose Cookie settings at the foot of any page of our website, or in its menu on a phone, and give a different answer; our planning experiences and workspace offer the same. If you withdraw your agreement, Google Analytics stops at once and we delete its cookies from your browser. Clearing your browser's cookies also clears your answer, and saying no changes nothing else about how the site works.

11. Retention

We retain different categories of personal information for different periods, depending on why we need the information and whether it remains part of an active account, plan, review, enquiry, or collaboration feature.

Account and profile records

We retain account credentials, profile details, and core account records while your account is active. If you delete your account, we generally delete or de-identify this information from active systems within 30 days, unless we need to retain it longer for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution. Encrypted or access-restricted backups may remain for up to 90 days before they are overwritten.

Trip plans, trip reviews, trip moments, chats, and other user content

We retain trip plans, reviews, moments, messages, prompts, and similar user-generated content while it remains in your account or in a shared plan, review, or collaboration space. If you delete this content, or if it is removed as part of account deletion, we generally remove it from active systems within 30 days. Content that is part of a shared plan, shared review, or conversation history may remain available to other participants until the shared item itself is deleted. Backups may persist for up to 90 days.

Uploaded media

We retain uploaded photos, videos, thumbnails, and related media metadata while the media remains attached to your account content. If media is deleted by you or as part of account deletion, we generally remove it from active systems within 30 days, subject to backups for up to 90 days and longer retention where reasonably necessary for abuse prevention, legal claims, or compliance.

A travel document you upload into a conversation is kept with that conversation, and is deleted with it when you ask us to delete your information.

Enquiries

An enquiry, meaning your contact details, the conversation and the trip summary, is kept for 24 months from when you sent it and then deleted, or sooner if you ask.

Planning conversations

If you plan a trip without an account, your conversations and what we recorded about your trip are deleted automatically once your anonymous session has gone 90 days without activity, unless you uploaded a file or saved a plan: then they are kept, with the file and the plan, until you ask us to delete them. If a conversation became an enquiry, the copy of it in the enquiry is kept as set out above.

Analytics

Google Analytics keeps the events it receives for 14 months. Your answer to our analytics question is kept in the tc_consent cookie for 6 months.

Invitations, notifications, recommendation feedback, and operational interaction records

We generally retain invites, notification records, recommendation feedback, and similar operational interaction records for 24 months after creation or resolution, unless a longer retention period is reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, or legal claims.

Security, fraud-prevention, and technical logs

We keep technical and security logs for short, fixed periods: our application's event and error logs for 3 days, the request and platform logs our hosting and database providers keep for us for up to 7 days, and the counters we use to limit how often our services can be called, which are keyed to a network address or an account, for about 2 minutes. A diagnostic record of how our assistant produced a reply, which includes the conversation it answered, is deleted no more than 31 days after that conversation's last message, or with the conversation if that is sooner. The record of sign-ins, sign-outs, password resets and session refreshes that our authentication provider keeps in our database is kept for 90 days, and a person's entries in it are deleted sooner: within a day of their account being deleted, and when we delete their information at their request. It holds a registered account's email address and, occasionally, the network address a request came from; an anonymous visitor appears in it only as a random identifier.

Legal, compliance, and dispute records

Where necessary, we may retain relevant information for the duration of a legal claim, investigation, audit, enforcement matter, or compliance obligation, and for any additional period required or permitted by applicable law.
