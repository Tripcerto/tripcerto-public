At the sizes they are met at

Rules

Primary identifierThe wordmark. The monogram is for compact use only, never where the wordmark fits.
Clear spaceThe diameter of the dot on the i, on all four sides.
Minimum sizeWordmark 120px wide, 72px at a push. Below that, the monogram, which floors at 16px.
FillsInk #281131 on light grounds, white on ink, on primary and on the band. No third fill.
Corner radius19.2% of the tile, carried in the artwork. Do not re-round it: iOS and Android mask on top.
One artwork per markNo separate small-size drawing. The rows above are the same file at different widths.

Never

  • Redraw, restretch, rotate or condense a mark. Scale only.
  • Recolour beyond the two fills. No gradient inside a mark, no outline, no drop shadow.
  • Set the wordmark in Instrument Sans or any other face.
  • Place a mark on a photograph, or on a ground that drops it under 3:1.
  • Lock a mark to a tagline or product name as fixed artwork.

02 · The band

A brand element, not a decoration. The Ember ramp with the colour itself warped and folded by noise, so it moves. It carries the hero, the close and the app icon.

dark mode

Parameters

Stops#E8437E → #FF5C6C → #FF7A5C → #FF9B7A
Angle100°, so the ramp runs left to right and a shade upward.
Warp0.25. How far the noise pushes a point along the ramp, in ramp lengths.
Fold size4.7 across the box's longer side, so a wide band and a tall one fold at the same physical size. The app icon uses 1.7, because a square tile takes more of the field.
Speed1. It never rests on a frame.
NoiseThree octaves of 2D simplex warped into each other: a base at 0.9, a detail octave at 1.8 displaced by the base, and a slow fold at 0.6 displaced by the detail.

What rolls, and what does not

Every load draws a different band. Reloads that showed the same folds in the same places read as a static image.

RollsSeed, anywhere in the field. Bias, up to a fifth of the ramp either way. Angle, ±25°. Warp, 0.85 to 1.15 of 0.25.
FixedStops, fold size and speed. These are the brand; the rest is weather.
PinnedThe app icon and this document cannot roll, so they name a seed: 41.1,87.9.

Without the shader

The still strip underneath, which is also what renders with no WebGL and under reduced motion:

background: linear-gradient(100deg, #e8437e 0%, #ff5c6c 50%, #ff9b7a 100%);

Three stops, not four. The still drops #FF7A5C, which exists to give the shader a longer run through the coral before the peach.

Surfaces and rules

bg-band-frostedWhite at 66% over the band, where a real backdrop filter would show another frame through.
bg-band-smokedInk at 76% over the band, the same for dark mode.
Dark modeAn ink wash at 55% over the live mesh, so the band sits with the dark page instead of glowing off it.
Copy on itWhite, and nothing smaller than a lede. White on the band is 3.00:1.
NeverThe band is a ground, not a fill. It does not go inside a mark, a letterform or a glyph. Do not restop it, add to it, or reverse it: the direction is pink to peach.

05 · Voice

One voice. The site talks to a buyer, Stella talks to a traveller or a consultant, and the rules below are the same for both. Name the work, the problem or the outcome. Never lead with a category.

Every rule is a swap

NeverInstead
Category language. the intelligence layer, AI-powered, seamless, end-to-end, unlock, orchestrate, transform travel, chatbotWhat happens, and for whom. Engage learns what each visitor wants, so your team can sell it sooner.
Saying what a thing is not. Not a chatbot, a workflow. X rather than Y.Say what it is, once, and move on.
An em dash or a bracket carrying a punchlineA comma, a full stop, or a second sentence.
A headline that instructs the buyer. Turn your website into…The software as the subject. Workspace builds the quote-ready trip.
A result no pilot has measured, or a third-party figure presented as oursWhat the software does. Nothing about how well it does it.
A wall of textTwo or three sentences, then stop.
A menu of questionsOne real question, or none.
Naming the machinery. Tools, identifiers, tags, (Noting: …)The trip, the work, the outcome. Never what is carrying it.

Two of these are enforced. A test fails the build if any banned word appears anywhere on a page, or if a heading opens with an instruction to the reader.

What we always say

  • Two products, two jobs. Engage turns website research into qualified inquiries. Workspace turns a travel requirement into a quote structure.
  • Works with the systems they already run, and never replaces them.
  • The expert decides what reaches the customer.
  • Plain words for anything technical, in the same sentence.

The shape of it, measured on the live site

Sentence lengthMean 10.9 words, median 9, across 268 sentences on the live site. Over 20 is an outlier.
PunctuationNo em dashes. No en dashes. No exclamation marks. No question marks. One semicolon on the whole site.
Personyou and your 104 times. we and our only where Tripcerto commits to something itself.
TensePresent. One future tense exists on the site, and it is a promise about a certificate we do not hold.
HeadlinesTwo to sixteen words, median nine, opening with a concrete subject.

Stella has three registers

DefaultWarm, curious, calm and clear, like a knowledgeable friend. Leans into substance and takes the room for it.
GuideA good guidebook: practical, specific, grounded, never salesy. Where the knowledge base does not cover something she says so plainly rather than inventing an answer.
ConsultantEconomical. A short precise reply is right far more often than a long one. She takes what she is told at face value and records it without restating it.

Everything above still applies to her. The registers change how much she says, never what she is allowed to say.

06 · Files

Markspublic/brand/ carries the wordmark, monogram and app icon, each as SVG in ink and in white. One path per mark, cut by the even-odd fill rule. Drop that rule and the counters fill solid.
Iconspublic/ carries favicon.svg, favicon.ico at 48/32/16, apple-touch-icon at 180, icon-192, icon-512, two maskable icons for Android, mask-icon.svg for a pinned Safari tab, and og-image at 1200 × 630.
Icon groundAll of them regenerate from one command, so a favicon cannot drift from an app icon: node scripts/og/build-icons.mjs --ground mesh --scale 1.7 --seed 41.1,87.9
Coloursrc/index.css declares the brand colours, the working set and the band, as CSS custom properties. That file is the source; this document reads it.
The bandsrc/components/ui/gradient-mesh.tsx is the shader, about 6 kB of WebGL with no dependency. src/components/site/Band.tsx passes the live parameters.
TypeInstrument Sans and JetBrains Mono, both from Google Fonts. Nothing to license.

Replace an artwork file, run node scripts/brand/build-brand-kit.mjs, and this whole document follows. A test fails the build if it stops matching the site.
